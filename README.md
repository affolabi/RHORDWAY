## Information Collation & PDF WhatsApp Flow

Users can reserve their travel slot through the interactive collation module:
1. **Pick Holiday Package & Departure Date**:
   - Doha + Zanzibar (Dec 12–19, 2026 · 7 Nights · ₦4,350,000 / person)
   - Doha Grand Prix (Nov 25 – Dec 1, 2026 · 6 Nights · Double ₦3,400,000 / Single ₦4,150,000)
2. **Collate Guest Information**:
   - Lead Traveller Name, WhatsApp/Phone number, Email, Departure City, Number of Travellers, and Special Requests.
   - Live booking calculation updates automatically (e.g. 2 guests × ₦4,350,000 = ₦8,700,000).
3. **Generate Official PDF Dossier**:
   - Generates an official, branded **Rhordway Reservation Dossier PDF** (`Rhordway_Reservation_[REF].pdf`) with unique Booking Reference, package inclusions, date stamp, and contact details.
4. **Send to WhatsApp Consultant**:
   - Automatically downloads the PDF to the guest's device.
   - Redirects to Rhordway’s official verified WhatsApp line (**+234 816 420 6030**) with a pre-filled reservation dossier ready to send.
